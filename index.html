    DEUS ORDO means Divine Order. We’re building a men’s brotherhood across races, ethnicities, nationalities, cultures, professions, and economic and social backgrounds. We welcome men of any faith or no faith. Members commit to shared standards of conduct while keeping their religious, political, and cultural beliefs.

    Our Foundation

    Different backgrounds. Shared principles. One brotherhood.

    Catholic moral and social teaching inspires our approach to human dignity, the common good, solidarity, subsidiarity, justice, and stewardship. These principles guide how we treat people, make decisions, and serve communities.

    Read our ten principles
    • Human Dignity. Every person has inherent worth and deserves respect.
    • Integrity. Act honestly, keep commitments, and reject deception, corruption, and exploitation.
    • Responsibility. Own your actions and fulfill your obligations at home, at work, and in the community.
    • Brotherhood. Support one another through cooperation, accountability, mentorship, and practical help.
    • Service. Put our skills and resources to work for others.
    • Justice. Treat people fairly, protect the vulnerable, and prevent unjust harm.
    • Stewardship. Use money, time, opportunities, and shared resources responsibly.
    • Subsidiarity. Make decisions at the level closest to the people affected whenever they can act effectively.
    • Solidarity. Take responsibility for one another and work for the common good.
    • Moral Accountability. Our principles take precedence over financial opportunities, majority votes, and personal interests.

    Build Together

    We want members to bring forward business ideas, offer professional expertise, make introductions, and mentor aspiring entrepreneurs.

    Proposals should first be reviewed for fairness, responsibility, and their effect on others, followed by a review of legal and practical risks. Any collaboration that proceeds should have clear terms and responsibilities.

    Shape Our Future

    We want members to help shape community projects, events, programs, and shared resources. Proposals must meet our foundational principles and applicable law before they can move forward. Formal voting procedures require separate approval.
