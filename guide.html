Community service guide

Turn a community request into a defined service project that the people using it can check and maintain.

Example project · Organize supplies at a community organization.

In order
  1. Listen and define the need
  2. Plan the work, people, and materials
  3. Run the session
  4. Verify and hand over
  5. Maintain and repeat what worked

1. Listen and define the need

Ask the organization which supplies it struggles to find, who uses them, and what it can maintain. Ask who else is affected. Agree on one shelf or room for the first session and how staff will confirm the result.

Rotary’s published experience emphasizes local ownership and budgeting for sustainability before implementation. Use a shared plan and a named receiving owner. Rotary’s lessons on lasting impact · Community assessment tools (PDF)

2. Plan the work, people, and materials

Name the organization’s point person, the team lead, and volunteers for sorting, labeling, and checking. Agree on access, disposal rules, lifting limits, supplies, and the stop time. With six volunteers working three hours, the example uses 18 volunteer hours. $24 in labels and $36 in bins = $60 cash before other costs. Keep donated labor separate.

3. Run the session

  1. Walk through the workspace with staff and confirm what can be moved.
  2. Label locations and list usable items by category and location. Flag unknown or unsafe items for staff.
  3. Leave the agreed workspace ready to use and show staff how to edit the list.

4. Verify and hand over

Ask staff to locate five items using the inventory. If four are found in the agreed time, record 4 ÷ 5 = 80% for that sample and fix the missing location. Save the final list where staff can reach it, confirm who owns it, and agree on the follow-up date.

5. Maintain and repeat what worked

At follow-up, ask whether staff can still find items and edit the list. Record the result, actual hours and materials, and any problem needing another session. Share only approved public outcomes. Leave identifying records with the organization.
